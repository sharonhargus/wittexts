Mabel Forsythe (Timber Wolf)

Interview by Lillian Morris

November 12, 2008

hidden

Mabel and Lillian were recorded in Witsët at Lillian's house by Sharon Hargus. This interview is a follow-up to an interview of Mabel in 2007 by Sharon (when no Witsuwit'en speaker was available).

This text was mostly translated by George Williams, Lillian Morris and Sharon Hargus, and mostly transcribed by Sharon Hargus (with Lillian's help in places).
